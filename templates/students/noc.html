APPROVAL PROCESS

How Your NOC Is Processed

Both TPO and Authority approval are required.

01

Request Submitted

Your NOC request is sent to the TPO for review.

02

TPO Approval

TPO reviews your request and supporting documents.

03

Authority Approval

After TPO approval, the request goes to the Authority.

04

NOC Approved

NOC becomes approved only after both approvals are completed.

Important

Submitting a request does not mean your NOC has been approved. The request must be approved by both the TPO and the Authority.
